# Code of Conduct

pyfect follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept feedback on the work, not the
person, and keep harassment of any kind out of every project space.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@tylergibbs1](https://github.com/tylergibbs1). Reports are kept confidential. The
maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may ban
anyone who keeps breaking it.
